ABOUT BOXOFFICEX

Movie Box Office, Made Easier to Explore.

BoxOfficeX is an independent movie information platform built to make box-office collections, movie records, actor careers, rankings and comparisons easier to discover and understand.

What is BoxOfficeX?

BoxOfficeX brings movie box-office information together in one place. Visitors can explore movie details, collections, verdicts, rankings and actor performance records without having to search through multiple pages or sources.

Focus on New Movies

One of the main goals of BoxOfficeX is to make new movie box-office tracking simple and useful.

New releases can include day-wise collection data, regional collection breakdowns and weekly totals so visitors can follow how a movie is performing after release.

What You Can Explore

🎬 Movie Details

Release information, budget, director, box-office totals and verdict.

📅 Daily Collections

Follow movie collections from Day 1 onwards with weekly totals.

🇮🇳 Regional Collections

Explore important regional markets including Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Telugu States and Rest of India.

🏆 Movie Rankings

Discover the highest-grossing movies and compare box-office performance.

🎭 Actor Profiles

Explore actor movies, blockbusters, hits, average performers, flops and highest-grossing films.

⚔️ Hero vs Hero

Select two actors and compare their movie careers and box-office performance.

Our Goal

The goal of BoxOfficeX is to build a clear, fast and easy-to-use movie box-office platform for cinema fans.

As the platform grows, BoxOfficeX aims to improve its movie database, collection tracking, actor records, rankings and comparison tools.

Independent Platform

BoxOfficeX is independently operated and is not an official website of any film studio, production company, distributor, actor or filmmaker.

Box-office figures and other movie information may be updated when newer or more reliable information becomes available.
